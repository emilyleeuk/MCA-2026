Reflection

I found that metadata can be represented in different ways in MEI. For example, I recorded MoVi and the Internationale Stiftung Mozarteum as information about my source, while the encoding date describes when I edited the digital MEI file. Using an authority record for Mozart also helped make the composer information more consistent.
