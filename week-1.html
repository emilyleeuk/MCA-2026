Theme

My chosen theme is Mozart's piano sonatas.

Challenges

From my own experience, music data can be difficult to organise because the same piece can have different scores, recordings and information attached to it, such as printed scores, digital scores, recordings and catalogue records. Access can also be a challenge as some music is freely available online while others may be restricted or only available through particular archives.

For Mozart's piano sonatas, the same piece can appear in different editions and recordings, and different websites or archives may describe the same work in different ways. This can make the information harder to compare.

Current Data Manifestations

Information about Mozart's piano sonatas can be found in online music archives, library catalogues, score databases and streaming platforms. These sources may include things such as titles, catalogue numbers, scores, recordings and performer information.

One strength of digital collections is that they make music easier to search for and compare. However, a weakness is that the information is not always presented in the same way across different sources, and some websites provide much more detail than others.
