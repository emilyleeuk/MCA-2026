Theme

My chosen theme is Mozart's piano sonatas.

Challenges

Music data can be difficult to organise because the same piece can have different scores, recordings and information attached to it. Access can also be a problem, as some music is freely available online while other material may be restricted.

Current Data Manifestations

For Mozart's piano sonatas, different archives may present and describe the same work in different ways. A strength of digital collections is that they make music easier to search and compare, but a weakness is that the information is not always consistent across different sources.
