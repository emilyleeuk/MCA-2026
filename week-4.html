Task 2: music21 Analysis

For this task, I used music21 to create different visualisations of my piece.

Piano Roll

The piano roll shows where different pitches appear throughout the piece. Most of the notes are in the middle register, with some higher and lower notes as well.

Pitch Histogram

The pitch histogram shows how often different pitches appear. It shows that notes in the middle register are used most often.

Pitch and Note Length

This graph compares pitch with note length. Most of the notes have shorter durations, while longer notes appear less often.
