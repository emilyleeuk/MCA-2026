\[Week 1- My Theme will be focused on Mozart's Piano Sonatas
Reflection- 
From my own experience, music data can be difficult to organise due to the same piece can have different recordings and information attached to it. Access can also be a problem, as some materials may be easy to access to but others may be restricted. 
For Mozart's piano sonatas, different archives may be present and describe the same work in different ways. A strength of digital collections is that they make music easier to search and compare, but weakness is that the information is not always consistent across different sources.

Week 2 
I found the transcription process quite time consuming, although adding the basic notes and rhythms in MuseScore was fairly easy once I got used to it. The more challenging part was adding extra notation such as articulations, trills and working with the treble and bass clefs. Some symbols were difficult to find at first, which slowed the process down.]
